# Romance of the Three Kingdoms
### A Graphic Novel Adaptation
**Original by Luo Guanzhong (trans. C.H. Brewitt-Taylor)**

*Art style: Ink-wash wuxia realism — a hybrid of traditional Chinese shuǐmò (water-ink) painting and cinematic Western graphic novel draftsmanship. Characters are rendered with meticulous, muscular linework in the tradition of classical Chinese figure painting, set against atmospheric, loosely-brushed backgrounds that echo Song Dynasty landscape scrolls. Dramatic chiaroscuro lighting carves heroes from shadow. Battle scenes employ explosive diagonal compositions with motion-blur ink streaks; intimate scenes use sparse, deliberate brushwork with deliberate negative space. The overall register is monumental and mythic — every panel should feel as though it could hang as a scroll painting, yet move with the kinetic urgency of a sword stroke. Think the visual vocabulary of Jae-Hwan Kim's 'King of Hell' fused with the atmospheric depth of Hokusai's wave compositions and the moral weight of classical Chinese court painting.*
