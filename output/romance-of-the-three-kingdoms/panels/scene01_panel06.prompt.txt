IMAGE PROMPT:
Wide-shot full-bleed borderless cinematic panel. Ink-wash wuxia realism, hybrid of classical Chinese shuǐmò painting and Western graphic novel draftsmanship. Song Dynasty atmospheric depth, Jae-Hwan Kim muscular linework.

COMPOSITION: The three sworn brothers stand shoulder to shoulder in the immediate foreground, backs fully to the viewer, silhouetted and three-quarter lit by the erupting sea of torchlight ahead. They occupy the lower-center third of the panel — three figures dwarfed by the vast, luminous scene before them, yet visually anchoring everything. The composition breathes outward from their silhouettes into an ocean of raised torches, illuminated faces, and falling peach blossoms. The horizon is high, leaving a sliver of ink-wash sky at the top edge where the first stars ignite against deep indigo.

CHARACTERS: Left to right — Liu Pei (lean, long-armed, jade-green and muted blue traveling robes, worn hem, heir's posture of quiet watchfulness even in celebration), Kuan Yu (tallest by a clear head, towering dark-silhouetted presence, deep crimson-black armor, long flowing beard catching the upward firelight in warm amber, one hand resting on the long shaft of the Black Dragon glaive whose blade casts its own deep shadow upward), Chang Fei (shorter, stockier, compact volcanic silhouette in earth umber, eighteen-foot spear angled diagonally upward-left, creating a compositional axis that electrifies the left edge of the frame). Their backs communicate both intimacy and the weight of what they have just made themselves responsible for.

ENVIRONMENT: The peach garden is ablaze with warm Imperial Gold and Jade Green. Every tree lit from below by torches, blossoms cascading everywhere like soft white snow in the warm updraft of three hundred flames. The crowd fills the middle and far distance — foreground villagers rendered as detailed joyful faces, mouths open, torches thrust skyward, expressions of collective release and relief; middle distance becomes detailed warm silhouettes against firelight; far distance dissolves into an abstract ink-wash tide of gold and ochre flame. The orchard canopy arches cathedral-like overhead, branches backlit and glowing against the ink-washed indigo sky. A scattering of white peach petals drifts across the viewer's plane of vision. At the sky's upper edge, three first stars appear — bone-white pinpricks against the deepening darkness.

LIGHTING: Dominant light source is massed firelight — warm orange-gold casting upward illumination that gilds the undersides of peach branches, halos the brothers from behind, and sculpts the crowd's faces with flickering warmth. The brothers' backs receive the same warm backlighting that the crowd faces toward. Color temperature: uniformly warm gold-and-jade, heroic and expansive. No cold shadows — this is the one uncomplicated moment.

MOOD: The great exhale. The brief golden interval before everything becomes war. Monumental warmth, collective breath released after sacred compression. The peach blossoms falling everywhere — the Brotherhood Marker woven into the very atmosphere. Elegant, elegiac, luminous.
