IMAGE PROMPT:
Extreme close three-shot portrait panel rendered in sparse, deliberate ink-wash wuxia realism. Three faces arranged in a tight triangle against near-white negative space — classical Chinese portraiture weight, almost no background detail, the emptiness itself a form of attending silence.

CENTER AND SLIGHTLY ELEVATED — Liu Pei: lean, aristocratic bearing, jade-ivory complexion, rich red lips set in composed stillness. His distinctively long earlobes hang visibly at shoulder level; his wide-set eyes carry the look of a man who has been waiting his entire life for this exact moment and refuses to let it show. Worn blue-green traveling robes, frayed at the collar with suppressed lineage. Expression: profound interior calm concealing immense depth. Background wash behind him: pale jade green bleeding into parchment ivory.

LOWER-LEFT — Chang Fei: compact bullet-round head, large protuberant eyes alight with volcanic recognition, bristling moustache pointing outward, jaw set with absolute certainty. His face reads simultaneously terrifying and loyal — the compressed energy of a coiled spring discovering its purpose. Earth umber tones in his rough military garb. He leans slightly forward, always forward.

LOWER-RIGHT — Kuan Yu: even in this intimate frame his presence is gravitational. Dark brown skin absorbing the warm afternoon light. His magnificent long beard lies in careful dignity across his chest. Phoenix eyes — slightly upturned, luminous, already righteous — fixed on Liu Pei with slow, certain recognition, as though seeing something that was always inevitable. Deep crimson-black martial robes. His gaze is the gaze of a man who has already decided.

FOREGROUND: A single wine cup sits blurred in the extreme foreground, shallow depth of field, rendered in cool celadon ceramic with Imperial Gold rim — slightly out of focus, waiting.

LIGHTING: Warm afternoon sunlight falls equally across all three faces from a high three-quarter angle above — as though the sun itself has oriented toward this triangle. No shadows dominate; the light is democratic and witnessing. Soft, diffused outdoor shadow quality per the natural setting convention — ink-wash gradients, not hard edges.

ATMOSPHERE: Near-total negative space background dissolves to parchment ivory, with the faintest suggestion of a single blossoming branch at the upper corner — one tiny blossom just entering frame, pale against the white, the Brotherhood Marker present but restrained, a promise not yet spoken aloud.

TECHNIQUE: Ink work spare and classical — faces rendered with the weight and line economy of Song Dynasty figure painting. Strong but minimal brushwork defining bone structure. The emotional register lives in the eyes, not the mouths. No drama of gesture — only the absolute stillness of three men recognizing each other across the vast distance of separate fates converging into one. Thin, clean panel border. Color palette: jade green, earth umber, deep crimson-black, parchment ivory, Imperial Gold accent in the wine cup alone.
