IMAGE PROMPT:
Full double-page bleed spread, no borders, image bleeds to all four edges. Ink-wash wuxia realism fused with cinematic graphic novel draftsmanship — meticulous muscular linework on foreground figures, atmospheric loosely-brushed Song Dynasty landscape technique for the receding army and sky.

COMPOSITION: Asymmetric. Three mounted brothers anchored lower-left foreground, army of five hundred sweeping diagonally right and deep into the middle distance, dissolving into morning mist at the horizon. The intimate and the vast held in a single frame, visual tension never resolved.

FOREGROUND — THREE BROTHERS, MOUNTED AND ARMORED: Liu Pei at center on a pale grey-white horse, lean and aristocratic in military robes with jade-green and muted Imperial Gold trim, his extraordinarily long arms visible at the reins, wide-set eyes lifted toward the horizon, mouth slightly open in address, expression of immense quiet resolve. At his hip, twin swords sheathed. His long ear-lobes at shoulder level. Kuan Yu to his right on a deep red roan stallion — towering above the others, dark brown skin catching the golden horizon glow from below, magnificent long beard flowing in faint dawn wind, phoenix eyes luminous and upturned, deep crimson-and-black lacquered battle armor, the Black Dragon glaive held upright in his right fist like a war-standard, its midnight blade catching bone-white light, casting its own deep shadow even in this golden dawn. Chang Fei to Liu Pei's left on a black stallion — compact, volcanic, bullet-round head, protuberant eyes blazing with restrained fury, earth-umber and deep brown armor worn rough from use, his eighteen-foot spear angled skyward at a bold defiant diagonal that crosses toward the upper-right of the composition, electrifying the frame.

ALL THREE LIT FROM BELOW by warm pale gold horizon light — faces resolute, upward, carved from the departing darkness above. A single blossoming peach branch detail, barely visible, woven into the lower-left corner of the composition — the Brotherhood Marker, the moral compass made visible.

ARMY: Behind the brothers, five hundred infantry and scattered horsemen fill the right two-thirds and middle distance. First plane — individual figures detailed, faces legible, ochre and earth-umber tones, wooden spear-hafts, cloth headbands. Middle distance — detailed silhouettes with color-block identification, forms compressing. Far distance — pure ink-wash abstraction, figures becoming suggestions of men, dissolving into pale morning mist. A tide of human will returning to vapor.

ATMOSPHERE AND SKY: Pale gold band of first light breaks along the entire horizon — warm Imperial Gold (#C9963D) bleeding into Yellow Ochre at the earth line. Above it, the sky deepens through blue-grey into the deep blue-black of departing night at the upper edge. The brothers glow against this gradient, lit from below like men standing at the edge of a burning world.

MOOD: Monumental and elegiac. The specific sorrow and beauty of the impossible chosen anyway. Every brushstroke carries scroll-painting weight and sword-stroke momentum.
