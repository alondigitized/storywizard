IMAGE PROMPT:
Near-aerial establishing shot, slightly tilted axis, full panoramic width. The composition divides brutally: upper two-thirds consumed by a vast Song Dynasty ink-wash sky — deep black ink bleeding into layers of indigo and midnight, the Milky Way rendered as a pale, diffuse brushstroke smear dissolving into nothing, cold white stars scattered as sparse, irregular flecks of bone white against absolute darkness. No warmth. No divine mercy in this sky.

The lower third: a dense river-bank wilderness of tall reeds and grass rendered in compressed, layered ink washes — ink-black at the roots, feathering to grey-green at the swaying tips, the blades overlapping in dark organic chaos. The river itself is a flat, lightless void at the far right panel edge — no reflection, no movement, just the annihilating absence of water merging with night.

Buried within the grass, almost lost entirely, two tiny child figures — the young Emperor and the Prince of Chen-liu — are curled together in tight fetal closeness, pressed against each other in the extreme lower-left third of the frame. Their torn silk robes — once pale ivory and muted imperial blue — catch the faintest cold starlight at the edges, barely distinguishable from the surrounding darkness. They are the smallest possible human note in an enormous indifferent composition. Their bodies are barely resolved — the suggestion of two small forms, the glint of pale fabric, the shape of children clinging together.

Scattered across the scene — hovering above the grass line, drifting toward the children in loose, irregular constellation clusters — fireflies rendered as individual pinpoints of Storm Teal (#1E6B7A) light edged with warm gold, each one a tiny luminous mote. They do not form a neat path; they gather in soft organic drifts, their collective light casting the faintest teal-gold illumination across the nearest reed tips and the children's upturned faces.

No hard panel borders. The image bleeds at all edges — top, sides, and bottom dissolving into darkness as if the panel itself cannot contain this much sky.

Art style: Ink-wash wuxia realism — shuǐmò atmospheric technique for sky and environment, minimal hard linework, maximum tonal gradation. Backgrounds are pure gestural brushwork. The children, though tiny, are rendered with delicate but precise linework to register as human and specific. Dramatic negative space is the primary compositional tool; the vastness of empty ink IS the subject.

Color palette: Near-monochromatic — deep blacks, indigo-midnight blues, cool ash greys for sky and water. Grass in muted olive-grey. The children's fabric in dim ivory and pale teal-grey. The only warm tones are the firefly motes themselves — points of Storm Teal-Gold (#1E6B7A / #C9963D hybrid) light that read as the only mercy in the frame. Color temperature: arctic cold everywhere except the fireflies.

Mood: Cosmic indifference made visual. Two children swallowed by the world. The beauty of the scene inseparable from its terror.
