IMAGE PROMPT:
Half-page vertical panel. Dramatic wuxia ink-wash realism in the tradition of classical Chinese shuǐmò painting fused with cinematic graphic novel draftsmanship.

COMPOSITION: The Black Dragon glaive — a massive midnight-black curved blade with a bone-white blood-moon edge — cuts a sweeping diagonal from upper-left to lower-right, bisecting the entire panel as both compositional axis and narrative event. The blade casts its own deep shadow independent of ambient light. The rebel leader occupies the lower-right quadrant, his figure separated into two clean halves along a near-perfect vertical line — rendered not as gore but as architectural geometry, the way a column cracks: with terrible symmetry and a kind of formal inevitability. The cut reads as classical tragedy, not carnage — the broken silhouette of a man who chose incorrectly.

KUAN YU: Monumental at 8-head heroic proportion, towering above every other element in the panel. Dark brown skin absorbing light, radiating solidity and permanence. His long sage's beard streams perfectly horizontal in the momentum of his own stroke — wind made visible by a man's will. Phoenix eyes half-lidded, almost serene: the calm of absolute mastery, not anger. Silkworm eyebrows composed. His lacquered deep crimson and black battle armor has a ceremonial gravity despite its obvious function. His cinnabar red war-cloak billows behind him like an unfurling battle standard — a wing of pure martial authority. His horse beneath him is mid-stride, hooves barely touching earth, utterly unbroken in pace. Kuan Yu himself is carved from different material than the world around him — stone moving through water.

ENVIRONMENT: The background is pure atmospheric ink-wash — swirling battlefield smoke in layered ochre-grey tones, loose brushwork evoking Song Dynasty landscape technique. Impressionistic suggestions of chaos: indistinct soldiers dissolving into abstract masses of earth umber and ash, banners half-visible in smoke. The far distance is almost entirely tonal wash — armies rendered as ink-mass, not individual figures. This background chaos makes Kuan Yu's crisp, precise rendering more startling — he is hyper-real against an almost abstract field.

LIGHTING: Single light source from upper-left — harsh battlefield sunlight cutting through smoke, casting dramatic chiaroscuro. The bone-white edge of the Black Dragon blade catches the light in a streak of pure luminance. Kuan Yu's crimson cloak glows against the ochre-grey smoke. Deep Midnight Ink shadows pool beneath the glaive and along the horse's flanks.

COLOR: Restricted battle palette — Cinnabar Red (#C1392B) dominating Kuan Yu's cloak and armor details, Midnight Ink (#1A1A2E) in the blade body and deep shadows, Bone White (#E8DFD0) blazing along the cutting edge, Yellow Ochre (#D4943A) and Smoke Ash (#7A7A8A) in the background smoke. The rebel figure is rendered in Earth Umber — common material meeting uncommon fate.

MOOD: Monumental, elegiac, inevitable. The feeling of watching history make a decision it cannot unmake. No text, no lettering, no symbols.
