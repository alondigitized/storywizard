IMAGE PROMPT:
Large vertical full-bleed panel, no border. Camera positioned at low-mid distance, looking upward along the full vertical axis of the composition — the viewer is standing among the exposed rebel infantry as the sky unmakes itself above them.

COMPOSITION: Cascade dissolution from top to bottom. The upper two-thirds of the panel is consumed by the disintegrating phantom army — hundreds of ghostly cavalry and infantry figures caught mid-air in the precise instant of unmaking. Where cascading droplets of dark blood-rain strike the phantom forms, the figures shred apart in long ragged strips and torn fragments, exactly like wet paper ripped by rain — some strips still recognizable as limbs or armor, others already dissolving into loose gestural ink-wash as they scatter upward and outward. Tight, controlled linework for the nearest fragments; increasingly loose, brushy, almost calligraphic dissolution as the shredding reaches upward toward the storm. No figure is fully intact — this is the simultaneous unmaking of hundreds. The movement reads downward and outward, an explosion of unmaking.

The black storm-clouds in the upper corners are visibly retreating — thinning, pulling back, the Storm Teal energy draining from them like ink rinsed from a brush. The supernatural light is failing.

LOWER THIRD: Real rebel soldiers suddenly exposed. Five or six figures in the immediate foreground — Earth Umber armor, yellow headbands — caught in the moment of naked terror. Their borrowed phantom shields have vanished. Faces turned upward: wide eyes, open mouths in silent horror, hands raised instinctively against the rain of dissolving fragments falling on them. One man's hands are outstretched, a torn phantom fragment — still vaguely horse-shaped, already half ink-wash — dissipating between his fingers. Beyond them, middle-ground soldiers are rendered as detailed silhouettes, the mass of an army stripped bare.

LIGHTING: Supernatural Storm Teal draining from above, cold and retreating. Below, pale returning daylight — thin Parchment Ivory — beginning to assert itself at the panel's lower edges, the first honest light these soldiers have seen. The light itself is the victory. No warm tones yet — only the cold aftermath of the uncanny.

ATMOSPHERE: The battlefield air is thick with dissolving ghost-matter — loose ink-wash washes of pale grey-teal float across the mid-ground like dispersing smoke. Fragments of phantom soldiers rain downward as torn paper scraps: some still holding a soldier's silhouette, others pure abstract brushwork. The ground below shows scattered pebbles going still.

STYLE: Pure ink-wash wuxia realism. Supernatural scenes protocol in full effect — shadow geometry broken, no hard environmental lines in the upper dissolution zone, Storm Teal replacing all warm palette tones. Human figures are dwarfed by the scale of the cosmic event above them. At least one zone of pure gestural wash with no linework whatsoever — the disintegrating mass at peak dissolution. The panel should read like a classical Chinese scroll painting of apocalyptic weather, kinetic and monumental simultaneously. Midnight Ink deepens the retreating storm; Bone White highlights falling fragment edges.
