Fairy Dust Silver trails drift through the composition like disturbed breath. Peter's head is thrown back, mouth open in a crow of pure laughter, the laugh of someone who has just played the best joke the sky has ever seen. His face is blank with the specific bewilderment of a very small child who has just experienced something his mind cannot yet process. Wendy floats in the middle distance to the left, slightly behind and apart, positioned precisely on the cool side of the light divide. She is perfectly still in the air — all the flight motion has drained from her body. Her white nightgown (grass-stained at the hem, small tear at the left shoulder) barely stirs. Her large watchful eyes are fixed on Peter. Not frightened. Not delighted. Careful. Still. The face of someone who has just understood something new and has quietly closed a small door inside herself. Her expression is the emotional axis of the entire panel — everything else orbits it. Tinker Bell hovers at Peter's ear, palm-sized, skeleton-leaf dress catching the golden warmth she herself casts. Her expression is composed, unreadable, tilted toward faint satisfaction — the face of someone whose private prediction has just been confirmed. Her light is warm clear gold, illuminating Peter's laughing face and Michael's dangling form with a generous, uncomplicated glow that does not reach Wendy. Peter casts no shadow. Irregular, softly organic panel borders — the Neverland threshold fully crossed. No text, no lettering, no speech bubbles, no captions.
