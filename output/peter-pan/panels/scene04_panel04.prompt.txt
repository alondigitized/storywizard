IMAGE PROMPT:
Wide horizontal panel, low horizon line with vast night sky dominating the upper two-thirds. The composition is split along a diagonal thermal line: the left and center bathed in warm Nursery Amber and Tinker Bell Gold, the right receding into cool Wendy Periwinkle and blue-grey shadow.

At center-left, Peter Pan hovers at a casual tilt, one bare foot braced lightly against the trailing wisp of a passing cloud, posture utterly nonchalant — the body language of a boy who has done nothing unusual. His free hand grips Michael's ankle, dangling the small boy upside-down over an infinite drop, the night sky vast and indifferent beneath. Peter's face is thrown back in open, uninhibited laughter, pure delight, as though the void below is the best joke the sky has ever told. A warm golden halo — Tinker Bell's cast light intensified by proximity — rings both Peter and Michael, their figures luminous against the dark.

Michael hangs inverted, striped cream-and-blue pajamas ruffled against gravity, round face dazed, hair splayed upward, small arms dangling loosely. His stuffed bear, clutched in one hand, swings free. His expression is the transparent, unedited bewilderment of a very young child who has not yet decided whether this is fun or frightening.

In the middle distance to the right, Wendy floats alone, separated from the other two by a band of cooler air. She is still, arms slightly crossed at her waist, her white nightgown catching a wash of blue-grey that belongs to no other figure in the panel. Her face is the critical focal point of the emotional register: the relief of rescue has finished draining away, replaced by something colder, more precise, and more adult — a careful, watchful stillness. She studies Peter across the cold air. She has understood something. The golden light does not reach her.

Near Peter's ear, barely visible at the edge of the warm halo, Tinker Bell hovers — a luminous point trailing a faint sparkle-path, her dragonfly wings precisely rendered. Her miniature face, if readable at this scale, holds an expression of composed, unreadable satisfaction. Her light is clear gold, not jealous amber — calm, observant, almost pleased.

The night sky behind all three is deep Midnight Blue fading toward indigo, threaded with Fairy Dust Silver where their flight paths have disturbed the air. A suggestion of cloud-mass gives depth. No ground visible — only the void.

CHARACTERS IN SCENE — Peter Pan: small, lithe boy of twelve, permanent tan, luminous pupil-less eyes, leaf-and-spider-silk tunic in russets and deep greens, red-feathered cap, barefoot, no shadow cast; Tinker Bell: palm-sized, skeleton-leaf dress in olive-gold, precisely veined dragonfly wings, self-luminous warm gold light source. Michael Darling: youngest Darling child, round-faced, age six, cream-and-pale-blue striped pajamas, small stuffed bear. Wendy Darling: girl of thirteen, white cotton nightgown, side-plaited hair beginning to loosen, large watchful eyes, Wendy Periwinkle palette cooling to blue-grey shadow.
