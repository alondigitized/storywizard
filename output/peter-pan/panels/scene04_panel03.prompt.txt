IMAGE PROMPT:
Extreme vertical composition, tall portrait orientation, viewpoint plunging straight DOWN as if the viewer is tumbling. The panel is designed to swallow the reader into freefall.

**FOREGROUND — TOP OF PANEL:** Wendy's face fills the uppermost third, pressed close to the picture plane — a girl of thirteen or fourteen, side plait loosening in the wind, white cotton nightgown collar at her throat. Her expression is raw terror: mouth wide open in a soundless scream, large watchful eyes stretched to their limits, every composed watchfulness stripped away. Her outstretched arms reach DOWN from her face, grasping hands in extreme close-up in the immediate mid-foreground — pale fingers splayed against the void, reaching for something impossibly far below. Her nightgown hem shows a faint grass stain. Her periwinkle palette — soft grey-blue — bleeds toward silver in the cold rushing air.

**THE VOID — CENTER OF PANEL:** Nothing but the vertiginous column of night air between her hands and the ocean floor — deep Midnight Blue (#0D1B4B) atmosphere, cross-hatched with delicate fine linework suggesting distance and speed. Faint Fairy Dust Silver (#C8D8E8) trails streak vertically, the luminous residue of interrupted flight. The emptiness is the subject. The panel breathes falling.

**FALLING FIGURE — LOWER MID-PANEL:** Michael — small, perhaps six years old, round-faced — a tiny tumbling shape shrinking toward the depths, arms and legs splayed in complete helplessness, the unselfconscious abandon of a body surrendered to gravity. His cream and pale blue striped pajamas catch what little cold light exists — the sole warm, pale note in the depths below, bright against the indigo void. Beside him, also falling, his small stuffed bear spins loose — a separate tumbling shape, fractionally ahead of him, arms out, ears catching the wind. Both figures are small enough to register as shapes rather than full faces, their humanity preserved only in posture and the recognizable stripes.

**DEEP BACKGROUND — BOTTOM OF PANEL:** Dense watercolor ink wash in darkest Midnight Blue and near-black indigo — the ocean impossibly far below, textured with Studio Ghibli-precise luminous wave-caps rendered as silver-white marks in the deep darkness, vast and indifferent. Cross-hatching becomes denser and finer as distance increases, suggesting the atmospheric compression of infinite depth.

**LIGHTING AND ATMOSPHERE:** Cold, sourceless moonlight from above, casting blue-silver along Wendy's upraised hands and the pale stripes of Michael's tumbling pajamas. No warm tones — Nursery Amber entirely absent. The overall palette is Midnight Blue dominant, Fairy Dust Silver secondary, the pale cream of Michael's pajamas as the single warm accent in the depths.

**PANEL BORDER:** Slightly organic, softly irregular — a Neverland-sequence border, the straight London lines already fully dissolved into this curved, breathing frame.

**STYLE:** Studio Ghibli-inspired watercolor illustration — layered wash technique, luminous atmospheric depth, expressive ink linework over soft painted ground. The mood is pure vertiginous terror dissolving into awe.
