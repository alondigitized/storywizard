IMAGE PROMPT:
Tall vertical panel composition — extreme overhead plunge viewpoint looking straight down into vast, terrifying depth. Edwardian ink-and-wash graphic novel illustration, Arthur Rackham and Edmund Dulac linework with cinematic dark fairy tale sensibility.

TOP THIRD: Wendy Darling's face dominates the upper panel — a girl of thirteen, large watchful eyes blown wide with raw, unguarded terror, mouth open in a cry that fills her whole face. Her white cotton nightgown pristine, her side plait whipping loose in the rush of wind. Her complexion bathed in cold silver-blue moonlight. Her outstretched arms plunge from her shoulders toward the viewer and down into the panel's depths — hands spread wide, fingers straining, grasping at nothing, the gesture of someone reaching for a falling child they cannot reach. Her color palette is Wendy Periwinkle (#7B9DC4) and Fairy Dust Silver (#C8D8E8), moonlight cold on white cotton.

MIDDLE DEPTH: Open air, nightsky, vertiginous recession. Crosshatching builds dimension in the atmospheric middle distance — finely ruled receding lines suggesting enormous height between Wendy above and the ocean impossibly far below. The sense of an abyss yawning open.

DEEP IN THE DEPTHS: Michael Darling — a very small boy, six years old, cream-and-pale-blue striped pajamas — tumbling arms-and-legs-splayed in freefall, his small round face a pale dot with a mouth open wide. Beside him, spinning loose and away from his grip, his small stuffed bear — one arm flung outward as if also falling. These two figures are tiny against the vast darkness — the bear almost as large as Michael at this scale, both shrinking as they plummet. The white of Michael's striped pajamas is the only luminous note in the deep black, a pale scrap of light swallowed by darkness.

BACKGROUND / DEEPEST PLANE: The ocean. Midnight Blue (#0D1B4B) rendered in dense, heavy ink wash — layered tones building from deep navy to absolute black at the panel's base. The water is impossibly, vertiginously far below. No horizon visible. Just the vast, indifferent dark ocean spreading to all sides and down and down. Fine crosshatching over the ink wash creates dimensional shadow and the suggestion of ocean surface texture far beneath.

ATMOSPHERE: No warm gold anywhere. No Nursery Amber. This is a Neverland night panel — Midnight Blue dominant, Fairy Dust Silver in Wendy's nightgown and the faint starlight. The cold is palpable. Panel borders beginning to soften at the edges from straight London geometry — the transition to Neverland visible in the slightly organic framing. The overall composition should feel as though the viewer is also falling — the panel itself a plunge.

No text. No words. No lettering of any kind.
