IMAGE PROMPT:
Wide, low-angle shot from ground level — the viewer crouches hidden in dense Neverland undergrowth, ferns and dark roots framing the bottom edge of the panel. The pirate procession moves laterally through torchlit trees, seen from below and slightly behind the foliage, as though the audience is something small and still that the procession has not yet noticed.

Four scarred, tattooed ruffians haul a low curtained chariot forward by thick ropes — their bodies strained and leering, faces half-dissolved in shadow, eyes catching torchlight like animals glimpsed in headlights. The chariot is draped in worn dark fabric, its frame low and crude.

Reclining on the chariot in absolute, unhurried magnificence is Captain Hook: long black ringlets cascading over a deep crimson velvet coat trimmed with tarnished gold brocade, his long pale cadaverous face framed by a precisely kept Van Dyck beard. Two cigars smoulder simultaneously at the corner of his mouth, twin coils of smoke curling organically upward into the dark canopy. His iron hook rests across his knee — depicted with exceptional sharpness and detail, the single most precisely rendered object in the panel, catching the amber torchlight like a cold, brilliant star amid the murk. He reclines with the absolute ease of a king en route to a feast he has already won. His shadow falls vast and disproportionate behind him, belonging to myth rather than physics.

Waddling alongside the chariot, amiably indifferent to the menace of everything around him, is Smee — compact, ruddy-cheeked, spectacles perched on his broad nose, a short blade hanging at his belt with the casual domesticity of a pocket watch. His round face carries its usual pleasant, slightly vertiginous cheerfulness.

Behind them, a grotesque chorus of pirate shadow-faces recedes into deep darkness — silhouettes, glinting eyes, the occasional suggestion of a weapon's edge.

Torchlight burns amber-gold (#C8860A) in hot cones between the trees; beyond that radius everything collapses into deep Midnight Blue (#0D1B4B) and pure black. The composition is formally symmetrical despite the low angle — Hook commanding the frame's geometric center even from ground level. Neverland panel borders are organic and slightly irregular, the foreground foliage interrupting the lower edge.

CHARACTERS IN SCENE — Smee: Hook's Irish bosun — compact, ruddy-faced, cheerful in a way that is quietly vertiginous. Round apple-cheeked face of a country shopkeeper, small bright eyes behind old-fashioned spectacles. Slightly shorter and rounder than surrounding pirates. Standard pirate working clothes: striped knit cap, loose work shirt, heavy canvas trousers, practical boots. Expression almost never varies — the same amiable pleasantness in all contexts. Color palette: London Slate (#6B7280), Lost Boy Earth (#8B6914).

ART STYLE — Studio Ghibli-inspired watercolor illustration: soft luminous watercolor washes, delicate hand-drawn linework reminiscent of Hayao Miyazaki and Kazuo Oga. Characters with simple expressive faces and large emotive eyes. Rich atmospheric backgrounds. The overall feeling is nostalgic wonder painted with the beauty and melancholy of a world you can never quite return to.
