IMAGE PROMPT:
Full-width horizontal strip, wide establishing shot from slightly above and behind, looking down past the children's huddled silhouettes toward Neverland below. The island fills the lower three-quarters — closer now, its geography legible but drained of all warmth. The sky is deep indigo bleeding into cold slate, starless and heavy. The jungle reads as shadow-green crosshatched mass — dense, illegible, shut. The lagoon is dark silver, a mirror of cold light with nothing warm in it. Dominating the right middle-ground: the pirate ship at anchor — a dense black silhouette built entirely in heavy crosshatch, its rigging scratched like wounds across the indigo sky, tattered black flags hanging limp, the hull massive and low in dark water. A single grey thread of smoke rises from below, barely distinguishable from sky. The panel borders are noticeably thicker and darker than any preceding panel, ink bleeding slightly inward at all four edges as though the page itself is losing confidence.

The children cluster in the upper foreground, small against the vast cold below. Wendy's arm encircles Michael, her white nightgown — grass stain at the hem, small tear at the left shoulder, faint watermark at the skirt — pulled against the wind, her face turned downward in genuine, unperformed fear. John stands beside her, both hands gripping his oversized battered top hat, wire spectacles slightly askew, bravado visibly hollowed. Michael is pressed into Wendy's side, striped cream-and-blue pajamas catching the only warm light in the panel, stuffed bear clutched to his chest — his face the unguarded terror of a child who has gone too far from home.

Peter hovers slightly ahead and apart — the one still figure. He faces away from the viewer, looking down at the island below. His expression is subtly shifted from triumph to something quieter and private: not afraid, but knowing. His leaf-tunic reads in deeper russets in this cold light. He casts no shadow.

Tinker Bell is a single point of warm gold near Peter's shoulder — the sole warm light source in the entire composition. Her glow casts golden undershadow across Peter's jaw and the children's upturned faces: a small, surrounded warmth. Her light is steady and watchful, not joyful.

CHARACTERS: Peter Pan — lithe boy of twelve, organic leaf-and-forest tunic in russet-deep-green, red-feathered cap, barefoot, no shadow present, luminous pupil-less eyes. Tinker Bell — palm-sized incandescent figure with precisely veined dragonfly wings, skeleton-leaf dress in olive-gold, sole warm light source casting golden undershadow on nearby figures. Wendy Darling — white cotton nightgown with grass stain at hem, left-shoulder tear, faint watermark, loosening side plait, periwinkle palette. John Darling — oversized increasingly battered top hat, wire-rimmed spectacles askew, Edwardian nightclothes. Michael Darling — striped cream-and-pale-blue pajamas, stuffed bear clutched tight. ART STYLE: Edwardian ink-and-wash, Arthur Rackham and Edmund Dulac linework, organic gestural Neverland mark-making, heavy crosshatching for dimensional shadow, irregular darkened panel borders with ink bleed at edges, Midnight Blue dominant palette with Tinker Bell Gold as sole warm accent throughout.
