IMAGE PROMPT:
Wide exterior shot, camera angle looking steeply upward from street level — the Edwardian terraced facade reduced to a narrow vertical wedge of London Slate brick at the image's lower edge, the nursery window a single warm amber rectangle set low in the composition. The building is small, intimate, almost consumed by an enormous Midnight Blue (#0D1B4B) night sky dominating three-quarters of the frame. Peter and Jane rise fast through the center — Peter in his leaf tunic of russet and viridian leads Jane by one extended hand, body angled forward into open sky, chin lifted, moving left-to-right with complete ownership of the night, never looking back. Jane, slightly above and behind his outstretched arm, flies with both arms flung wide, head thrown back, face lifted and laughing with her whole body — posture the precise mirror of a first flight, arms open to receive everything. Their clothing and hair trail in consistent upward wind-motion. Fairy Dust Silver (#C8D8E8) luminescence washes the air around both figures. High in the frame, a fading smear of Tinker Bell Gold (#F5C518) dissolves into the stars — the last trace of her light, nearly swallowed, silver-white at its extinguishing edge. Stars are dense luminous watercolor silver-white near the zenith, thinning toward a ghost of pale rose-gold at the far horizon — not yet dawn, perhaps only imagination bleeding into indigo. The nursery window below is open; warm amber light spills softly onto the surrounding brickwork. Framed within it: adult Wendy, small and composed in a simple periwinkle dress with London Slate undertones, one hand resting quietly on the open sill. She does not wave. She simply watches. Her face holds joy and grief in the same utterly composed expression. The window was always going to be open.

CHARACTERS IN SCENE — Peter Pan: A boy of about twelve, small and lithe, body assembled from stored kinetic energy. Face beautiful and slightly alien — large luminous eyes without visible pupils, sharp chin, mouth near a grin of mischief. Permanent tan. Tunic of overlapping autumn leaves — russets, deep greens, umber browns stitched with spider silk. Small red-feathered cap. Always barefoot. Small dagger tucked in tunic. Always moving left-to-right, visually refusing retreat. Dominant colors: Neverland Viridian (#1A7A5E), Fairy Dust Silver (#C8D8E8) in flight. | Tinker Bell: Present only as a near-extinguished trail of Tinker Bell Gold (#F5C518) dissolving into the upper stars, its edge fading silver-white — a sparkle-path becoming indistinguishable from starlight.

Art style: Edwardian ink-and-wash with luminous watercolor washes — pen linework inspired by Arthur Rackham and Edmund Dulac. London facade rendered with precise, architectural linework and controlled crosshatching. Figures in flight rendered with looser, more gestural marks. Shadows rich and ink-heavy. Warm amber window glow set against the vast cold indigo sky. Aesthetic: dark fairy tale — beautiful and elegiac, the slight oversaturation of a cherished memory meeting the dignified restraint of a London night. Final image of the adaptation: a storybook remembered rather than told for the first time.
