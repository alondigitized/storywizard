IMAGE PROMPT:
Full-width panoramic aerial view from slightly behind and below the flying children, looking outward and downward toward the horizon. The frame is vast and breathless — a sky that feels impossibly tall, the ocean spreading below into impossible distance. The sun hangs enormous and low at the horizon's center, a swollen disc of white-gold bleeding into the sky in long parallel shafts of light that pour across the composition like golden arrows, all converging on the island below. The light is physical, architectural — you can feel its weight.

Neverland rises from a dark sea in the lower third of the frame: jungle green of almost painful saturation, a lagoon of impossible turquoise catching the sun like hammered gemstone, a thread of smoke curling in lazy white from a hidden camp, and the tiny dark silhouette of a ship anchored in the bay, so small it could be missed. The island's colors are wrong in the way memory is wrong — too vivid, too themselves, the green too green, the water too blue, as though the pigments were applied by someone who loved it too much to be accurate.

Peter Pan is a black cutout silhouette at center-frame, suspended mid-flight against the blaze of gold, one arm raised and pointing directly down at the island below him. His red-feathered cap and leaf-tunic edges catch the gold backlight as a thin corona. He casts no shadow. His pose is absolute stillness within motion — he has stopped completely, the only fixed point in a world of wind.

Behind and above him, the three Darling children drift upward in loose formation — Wendy slightly ahead, John and Michael further back — faces all tipped toward the island below, expressions of dazed, aching recognition. Their nightclothes and hair stream in a consistent left-to-right wind. Wendy's nightgown shows the grass stain at the hem and the small tear at the left shoulder. John's top hat is askew. Michael's arms drift slightly outward, instinctively.

Tinker Bell is a nearly invisible point of Tinker Bell Gold (#F5C518), swallowed by the sun's blaze — only the faintest shimmer of her trailing sparkle-path distinguishes her from the light itself.

The sky is Fairy Dust Silver (#C8D8E8) bleeding into deep gold at the horizon. The ocean below is Midnight Blue (#0D1B4B) darkening away from the island. The panel border is organic and slightly curved — Neverland pressing against the frame's edges.

CHARACTERS IN SCENE — Peter Pan: small, lithe boy of about twelve, permanent tan, leaf-and-forest tunic in russets and deep greens, red-feathered cap, barefoot, no shadow, luminous pupilless eyes, small dagger visible at tunic. Tinker Bell: palm-sized, skeleton-leaf dress in olive-gold, precisely veined dragonfly wings, here visible only as a near-invisible luminous point against the sun's blaze. Wendy Darling: thirteen, white nightgown with grass stain at hem and tear at left shoulder, side plait loosening, watchful face tipped in wonder. John Darling: wire spectacles askew, oversized Sunday top hat tilted, earnest and slightly awed. Michael Darling: youngest, round-faced, striped cream-and-blue pajamas, arms drifting wide in pure unconscious wonder.

Art style: Studio Ghibli-inspired watercolor illustration — soft luminous watercolor washes with delicate hand-drawn linework reminiscent of Hayao Miyazaki and Kazuo Oga. Rich atmospheric backgrounds, water that shimmers and breathes, clouds with depth and movement. Oversaturated DAYLIT NEVERLAND palette pushed 20% beyond photographic realism. Magic rendered as soft golden light and floating particles. Nostalgic wonder — the beauty and melancholy of a world you can never quite return to.
