IMAGE PROMPT:
Wide establishing shot, low horizon line, maximum sky and water — a vast Neverland lagoon at dead of night stretching to the frame's edges, the world rendered in deep layered watercolor washes of black-green and bottle-glass, water dense and treacherous, breathing faint phosphorescence from below. At the composition's center-middle-distance, Marooners' Rock breaks the surface: a flat, indifferent shelf of dark stone barely clearing the tideline, its wet face pooling moonlight like spilled silver. Tiger Lily sits upright at the rock's exact center — wrists and ankles bound with coiled rope, water already lapping cold around her feet — but her spine is an arrow and her chin is raised, gaze fixed on the far horizon as though the ropes are beneath her consideration. A vast, swollen full moon dominates the upper third of the frame — enormous, slightly too yellow, the over-vivid gold of a memory rather than an observed thing, its reflection shattered across the lagoon's disturbed surface in ragged silver shards. Dense Rackham-inspired crosshatching builds shadow in the rock's underhang, that hatching layered into the watercolor washes so shadow has texture and weight. A low mist trails ghostlike across the water's middle plane, softening the dinghy's approach: Smee in the prow, round-faced and placid, another pirate pulling at the oars behind him — the small boat moving in from the right edge of frame, its silhouette dark against the broken moonlight. The lagoon's color world is pure Neverland night: Midnight Blue dominant, Neverland Viridian as an underpaint in the deepest water, Hook's Crimson bleeding faintly into the atmosphere immediately around Tiger Lily — danger and her own courage sharing the same hue. Crocodile Shadow as a subtle undertone in the rock's hatching, time patient in the water's rising. Organic, slightly irregular panel borders — a Neverland-night frame that curves almost imperceptibly, one wisp of mist interrupting the lower edge.

CHARACTERS IN SCENE — Tiger Lily: tall, absolutely still, high strong cheekbones, gaze of complete self-possession, head always erect — her visual signature even in captivity. Ceremonial dress in warm ochres and deep umbers with beadwork detail, structured feathered headdress intact. Her stillness reads as power, not defeat. Color palette: Lost Boy Earth, Hook's Crimson surrounding her, Neverland Viridian in her roots to the island. | Smee: compact, ruddy, round-faced, apple-cheeked, small bright eyes behind old-fashioned spectacles, striped knit cap, loose work shirt, heavy canvas trousers — his amiable pleasantness unchanged by context. Color palette: London Slate, Lost Boy Earth.

Art style: Studio Ghibli-inspired watercolor illustration — soft luminous washes, delicate hand-drawn linework in the manner of Miyazaki and Kazuo Oga. Water that shimmers and breathes. Meticulous atmospheric backgrounds. Characters with simple expressive faces and large emotive eyes. The world handpainted, layered, alive — nostalgic wonder threaded with the beauty and melancholy of a place you can never quite return to.
