Her white nurse's apron catches the only amber light reaching her — a last fragment of the warmth she is being expelled from.

DARLING stands very still at the threshold of the nursery doorway at frame-right — not the door Nana is being dragged toward, but the one behind her, which places her perfectly between the children and the scene. Art style: Studio Ghibli-inspired watercolor illustration — soft luminous watercolor washes with delicate hand-drawn linework reminiscent of Hayao Miyazaki and Kazuo Oga. Characters drawn with simple expressive faces and large emotive eyes. The world feels alive, layered, and handpainted. The overall feeling is nostalgic wonder — childhood adventure painted with the beauty and melancholy of a world you can never quite return to.
