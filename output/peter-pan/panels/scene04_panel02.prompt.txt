IMAGE PROMPT:
Medium shot suspended in open sky — the horizon line sits low, leaving three-quarters of the frame as pale gold and grey-blue watercolor wash, clouds dissolved into atmospheric suggestion rather than solid form. Center-frame, PETER PAN hangs completely inverted in mid-flight — back arched, feet lazily crossed at the ankle above him, one arm dangling with absolute feline indifference while the other hand closes triumphantly around a silver fish just plucked from a SEABIRD mid-wing. The bird wheels away toward the upper-right corner, feathers trailing in its wake, its beak open in an expression of pure comic affront, wings splayed in that unmistakable posture of a creature that cannot believe what just happened to it. Peter grins around his prize — teeth bright, chin tilted down toward the viewer since he is upside-down, hair wild and fanned outward by the slipstream, red-feathered cap somehow still clinging by sheer narrative conviction. His leaf-tunic hangs loose around his shoulders, Neverland Viridian and russet, no shadow falling from his body in any direction.

At the panel's mid-left, WENDY floats in her white nightgown — slightly further away, watching, her expression caught in the precise gap between delight and the first hairline fracture of unease. Her hands are loosely clasped at her waist, that characteristically attentive posture, but something in the set of her mouth carries a question she has not yet formed. Behind and to her right, JOHN hovers in nightclothes and oversized Sunday top hat — brow deeply furrowed, one hand raised toward his wire-rimmed spectacles as though verifying that what he is witnessing is, in fact, occurring. The hat sits slightly askew from wind-drag.

The light source is ambient diffused gold from an unseen low sun behind the viewer — warm, oversaturated just beyond what atmosphere would allow, the slightly-too-vivid quality of a remembered moment pressing against the edges of the panel. Fairy Dust Silver trails from the children's wake-paths, catching the gold and refracting it into soft luminescence against the blue-grey air. The panel borders are transitional: straight-edged at the left margin, beginning to soften and curve organically toward the right — the visual grammar of Neverland's world starting to reassert its dreamed instability. Linework throughout is loose, gestural, slightly irregular — not the architectural precision of London but not yet the fully breathing wildness of the island.

CHARACTERS: Peter Pan — boy of about twelve, small and kinetically assembled, luminous pupil-less eyes, permanent tan, barefoot, leaf-and-spider-silk tunic in russet and deep green, red-feathered cap, small dagger at his belt, scratched hands — Viridian and Fairy Dust Silver palette. Wendy Darling — girl of thirteen, composed prettiness, attentive watchful eyes, pristine white cotton nightgown, side-plaited hair lifting in flight-wind, Periwinkle palette. John Darling — stocky boy of eleven, squared face, wire-rimmed spectacles, nightclothes, oversized top hat, Slate palette.

ART STYLE: Edwardian ink-and-wash, Arthur Rackham and Edmund Dulac inspiration, organic gestural linework, rich crosshatched shadows, luminous watercolor, dark fairy tale aesthetic — beautiful, slightly unsettling, storybook-remembered.
