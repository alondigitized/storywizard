IMAGE PROMPT:
Medium shot, open sky. Peter Pan floats inverted in mid-flight at the panel's center — completely upside-down, crossed bare feet toward the sky, one arm dangling loose with careless feline ease, the other hand raised holding a silvery fish he has just plucked clean from a large seabird mid-swipe. The seabird wheels away in the immediate foreground, wings splayed in undignified retreat, a cascade of white feathers spiraling outward in its wake — its expression magnificently, comically offended. Peter grins around his prize, wild hair falling upward with gravity reversed, utterly unbothered by the chaos he has just introduced into the natural order. His leaf-tunic fans slightly open as he hangs, his red-feathered cap somehow still attached. He casts no shadow — the pale sky behind him is uninterrupted, his silhouette clean and uncanny.

At mid-distance to Peter's right and slightly lower in the frame, Wendy and John float side by side, small enough to establish depth and height. Wendy's expression walks the precise knife-edge between genuine delight and the first shadow of unease — her mouth is almost smiling, her brow fractionally creased, the expression of someone who finds something wonderful and realizes the wonder might not be entirely safe. John, beside her, has both hands raised toward his wire-rimmed spectacles, adjusting them with a brow furrowed in official disapproval — the posture of a boy who has decided someone must take a position on stealing fish from seabirds in midair, and that someone is him. His oversized top hat is slightly askew from the wind. Wendy's white cotton nightgown is pristine, catching the silver-pale light, her side plait beginning to loosen in the open air.

The background is an open expanse of high atmosphere — a pale watercolor wash of cool grey-blue and muted gold where thin clouds dissolve into horizon haze. The light is soft and sourceless, neither morning nor evening, the sky of a threshold between worlds. Fairy Dust Silver traces the wake of all three children's flight paths — a luminous, faintly particulate trail hanging in the air. The panel border is straight but the barest softness has begun to enter its corners, the visual geometry of London starting to dream.

The linework throughout is loose and gestural — figures rendered with flowing, slightly abbreviated strokes that suggest movement rather than pinning it down. The composition is slightly tilted — not dramatically, but enough to feel alive, as though the air itself is unreliable.

CHARACTERS IN SCENE — Peter Pan: A boy of about twelve, small and lithe, assembled from stored kinetic energy. Face beautiful and slightly alien: large luminous eyes without visible pupils, sharp chin, mouth perpetually edging toward mischief. Permanent tan. Tunic of overlapping autumn leaves — russets, deep greens, umber browns — with red-feathered cap. Always barefoot. No shadow.

ART STYLE: Studio Ghibli-inspired watercolor illustration — soft luminous washes, delicate hand-drawn linework, rich atmospheric backgrounds. Characters with simple expressive faces and large emotive eyes. Fluid, dynamic poses. Nostalgic wonder painted with the beauty and melancholy of a world you can never quite return to.
