IMAGE PROMPT:
Low-angle wide establishing shot: the Jolly Roger at night, massive black-tarred hull filling three-quarters of the frame, the ship looming from slightly below and to the starboard side. Three cast-iron lanterns throw deep orange-gold pools across weathered hull planking and dark rippling water below. The crimson sails hang heavy at the upper frame edge. Cold silver moonlight touches only drawn steel.

Controlled chaos across the full composition: two Lost Boys in ragged animal-skin earth tones cling to rope-ladders on the hull, bodies straining upward mid-climb; a third vaults the deck rail in silhouette against lantern glow; a fourth trades sword-blows on deck with a bulk pirate, lit harshly from below by deck lanterns. The upper rigging is shadow-dense and alive with implied movement — ropes crossing the Midnight Blue sky, shapes half-resolved.

On deck midship, Wendy stands with wrists bound — her white cotton nightgown bearing all accumulated journey marks: grass-stain at the hem, small tear at the left shoulder, faint watermark, cook-fire scorch mark — posture unbroken, chin raised, entirely undefeated. Beside her, Smee hovers in visible confusion: compact, ruddy, apple-cheeked, his knife held at an undecided angle, round spectacled face wearing its habitual amiable placidness even as his eyes dart between too many threats at once.

Overall palette is deep orange-and-shadow: Midnight Blue (#0D1B4B) sky and ocean, Nursery Amber (#C8860A) in every lantern-pool, Hook's Crimson (#8B0000) in sails above. Moonlight catches only on metal — sword edges, a ship's bell, iron fittings. Hull woodwork and rigging rendered in meticulous Rackham-inflected crosshatching; water and smoke in loose, soft watercolor washes. Panel border organic and slightly irregular, characteristic of Neverland sequences.

CHARACTERS — Peter Pan: boy of twelve, lithe and perpetually in motion; leaf-and-forest tunic in russet and deep green, red-feathered cap, barefoot; luminous pupil-less eyes; small dagger at hip; shadow absent or misaligned. Dominant color: Neverland Viridian (#1A7A5E). Wendy: white nightgown with all accumulated journey details as described; loosening side plait; hands reaching or straining against bonds; unbroken composure. Dominant color: Wendy Periwinkle (#7B9DC4). Smee: compact and round, ruddy-faced; striped knit cap, loose work shirt, heavy canvas trousers; old-fashioned spectacles; expression of pleasant domestic calm wholly incongruous with context. Dominant colors: London Slate (#6B7280) and Lost Boy Earth (#8B6914).

ART STYLE: Studio Ghibli-inspired watercolor illustration — soft luminous washes with delicate hand-drawn linework in the manner of Hayao Miyazaki and Kazuo Oga. Characters with simple expressive faces and large emotive eyes, fluid dynamic poses. Night palette of deep indigo and silver with warm amber lantern accents. Every surface handpainted and alive. Nostalgic wonder undercut by genuine menace and real violence.
