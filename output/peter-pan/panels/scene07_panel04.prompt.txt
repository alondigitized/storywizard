IMAGE PROMPT:
Studio Ghibli-inspired watercolor illustration — soft luminous watercolor washes with delicate hand-drawn linework reminiscent of Hayao Miyazaki and Kazuo Oga.

Large establishing shot: the Jolly Roger viewed from slightly below and to the starboard side, the massive black-timbered hull rising like a cliff face and filling three-quarters of the frame. Precise Rackham-style ink linework structures every plank, rope-cleat, and carved gun-port on the hull and rigging; looser, softer watercolor washes dissolve into heaving water and drifting cannon smoke below. The night sky is deep Midnight Blue (#0D1B4B) with a partial moon casting cold silver light across drawn steel throughout. Orange-gold lantern pools (Nursery Amber #C8860A) spill across the dark water and climb the hull planking. Hook's Crimson (#8B0000) bleeds through half-furled sails above. Controlled chaos fills the composition: two Lost Boys cling to rope-ladders mid-climb on the hull; a third vaults the rail in a frozen burst of motion; a fourth on deck crosses blades with a pirate, swords catching cold moonlight. The rigging overhead is a dense web of shifting shadow — gestural and loose against the precisely rendered woodwork below. On deck: Wendy stands upright with wrists bound, nightgown luminous white against dark planking — hem grass-stained, left shoulder bearing a small tear, a faint watermark at the waist — face composed and unbroken, chin raised. Smee stands close beside her, round spectacled face swiveling with baffled alarm, knife extended but aimed at nothing certain, caught between threats arriving from too many directions. At the ship's shadowed prow, barely resolved from the dark water, a small silhouette begins to rise — Peter emerging from below, ascending the hull, casting no shadow against the planking. Organic, slightly curved panel borders consistent with Neverland sequence conventions.

CHARACTERS IN SCENE — Peter Pan: A boy of about twelve, small and lithe with a body that seems assembled entirely from stored kinetic energy — he is never quite still. His face is beautiful and slightly alien: large luminous eyes without visible pupils, sharp chin, permanently tanned skin. Tunic assembled from overlapping autumn leaves and forest materials — russets, deep greens, and umbers. No shadow cast by him; small dagger tucked at his leaf-tunic. | Smee: Hook's Irish bosun — compact, ruddy-faced, and cheerful in a way that should be reassuring and is instead quietly vertiginous. He has the round, apple-cheeked face of a country shopkeeper, small bright eyes behind old-fashioned spectacles. Standard pirate working clothes — striped knit cap, loose work shirt, heavy canvas trousers, practical boots. Domestic props at violent odds with his context; spectacles giving him an accountant's or schoolmaster's air; expression almost never varies — the same amiable pleasantness whether performing a domestic chore or an act of violence.
