IMAGE PROMPT:
Extreme close-up horizontal strip panel, ultra-wide aspect ratio spanning the full page width. Viewpoint from just below eye-level, the viewer standing directly in the path of Captain Hook's cold gaze — the reader becomes the next figure in his path.

COMPOSITION: Hook's face fills the full horizontal strip from edge to edge. His forget-me-not blue eyes — luminous, ice-cold, profoundly beautiful, almost Pre-Raphaelite in their precision — dominate the center of the frame. The iron hook occupies the extreme foreground, slightly out of focus, its polished iron surface catching a single cold glint. At the far left and far right edges of the strip, two small amber points of light hover at the panel border — the glowing tips of his double cigar — their faint smoke curling upward and out of frame.

CHARACTERS: Captain Hook, extreme close-up. Long, pale, cadaverous face. Precisely maintained jet-black Van Dyck beard. Ringlet curls of black hair — dense, black as candle wax — visible at the temples. His expression is the most technically controlled face in the entire graphic novel: not anger, not cruelty, not triumph — only the profound, untroubled boredom of a man for whom the world ceased offering surprises long ago. His blue eyes are the single warm note in his dark palette — forget-me-not blue, depicted with extraordinary care, luminous with cold intelligence, almost beautiful in their absolute disengagement. The lace cravat is visible at the lower frame edge: impossibly, miraculously white against deep crimson velvet.

ENVIRONMENT: Background is an out-of-focus wash of deep Midnight Blue (#0D1B4B) — the Jolly Roger's deck dissolving into nautical darkness, indistinct rigging and dark wood barely readable as shapes. No sky. No horizon. Only this face and its absolute self-possession.

LIGHTING: Cold, flat ship-deck light from above, falling precisely on his features. No warm sources except the two amber cigar-tip glows at frame edges. The iron hook catches whatever ambient light exists — a hard, singular glint. His shadow behind him extends disproportionately large into the Midnight Blue background, belonging to myth rather than physics.

STYLE: Studio Ghibli-inspired watercolor at maximum linework precision. This panel deliberately breaks from Neverland's organic irregularity — every line is hard-edged, architecturally exact, formally severe, the most controlled linework on the page. The composition is symmetrical, ceremonial. Hook's Crimson (#8B0000) velvet, glimpsed at the lower frame, rendered with careful attention to worn gold brocade detail. Color palette: Midnight Blue background, Crimson at edge, the forget-me-not blue of his eyes as the focal anchor, the white blaze of his cravat, twin amber cigar-glow at the panel margins.

EMOTION: The unsettling serenity of a man who has mastered every room he has ever entered. Profound boredom elevated to something regal and cold. Not a villain's rage — something worse. The absolute stillness of someone who has already decided. The adult world looking back.
