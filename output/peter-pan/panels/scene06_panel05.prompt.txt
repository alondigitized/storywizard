IMAGE PROMPT:
Near-full-width panel, low camera angle looking up from the waterline — Marooners' Rock rising from absolute black lagoon water, the moon vast and blazing white directly behind both figures, so enormous it fills nearly the entire upper half of the composition. For one suspended heartbeat, Peter Pan and Captain Hook are pure silhouette against that incandescent disc — boy shape and man shape, small and tall, wild and formal — before the scene erupts into kinetic action.

Peter is low and fast, body dropped into a fighter's crouch, bare feet gripping the rain-slicked rock face with animal certainty. His dagger extends forward, arm fully extended, his whole body a single diagonal line of forward momentum — he moves like water finding a crack in stone. His leaf-tunic catches fragments of moonlight at its edges. He has no shadow on the wet rock beneath him.

Hook presses forward from the opposite side, rapier thrusting, iron hook raised high and catching the moon's light like a mirror shard. His great crimson coat billows dramatically behind him, tails lifting in the sea wind. His disproportionately enormous shadow stretches away from him across the rock and into the water below, belonging to myth rather than physics.

The lagoon on every side is Midnight Blue (#0D1B4B), absolute and lightless, a flat black infinity broken only where the moon's reflection fractures in the disturbed water at the rock's base — shattered silver and white shards radiating outward from every wave the combatants generate. The rock itself sits isolated, barely large enough to contain them both, its wet surface rendered in dense Rackham-style crosshatching that builds the pooling shadows between their feet into something architectural and alive.

The panel border bleeds at the lower edge — the lagoon water runs directly into the gutter with no containing line, dissolving the boundary between fiction and void. Irregular organic border treatment consistent with Neverland night sequences.

Dominant palette: Midnight Blue lagoon and sky, blazing silver-white moon, Hook's Crimson catching the moonlight at coat-hem and hook, Fairy Dust Silver fragmenting in the water below. Peter's leaf-tunic holds a trace of Neverland Viridian even in silhouette, the living green refusing darkness.

CHARACTERS IN SCENE — Peter Pan: boy of about twelve, small and lithe, luminous eyes without visible pupils, beautiful alien face with sharp chin, permanent tan, barefoot; tunic of overlapping autumn leaves in russets, deep greens, and umber browns, small red-feathered cap; no shadow on the rock beneath him. Captain Hook: tall and cadaverous, long pale face, precisely maintained black Van Dyck beard, jet-black ringlet curls, deep crimson velvet coat with tarnished gold brocade trim — magnificent and fractionally decayed — iron hook replacing right hand, its shadow disproportionately vast.

Studio Ghibli-inspired watercolor illustration — soft luminous watercolor washes with delicate hand-drawn linework, fluid movement and dynamic poses, deep indigos and silvers for night sequences, the world feeling alive, layered, and handpainted with nostalgic wonder and an undertow of genuine menace.
