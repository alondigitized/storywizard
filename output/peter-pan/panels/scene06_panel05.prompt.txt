IMAGE PROMPT:
Near-full-width dramatic panel, camera at medium distance on a low angle looking up at Marooners' Rock — a wet, isolated stone platform rising from absolute black lagoon water that fills the entire frame perimeter. The panel border bleeds on all sides, dark water dissolving into the gutter with no containing line. An enormous full moon hangs dead-center and blazing overhead, cold-white and immense, filling a third of the sky — its face a merciless disk of light that reduces both combatants to bold silhouettes for one suspended beat before kinetic action resolves into detail.

The fight is pure explosive motion frozen mid-exchange. Peter is down low — nearly horizontal over the slick stone surface, bare feet splayed wide for grip, body torqued, small dagger extended forward at full arm's length in his right hand, left arm thrown back for counterbalance. He moves left-to-right with the momentum of water finding a crack in stone — irresistible, elemental, grinning. His leaf-tunic blazes with Neverland Viridian against the dark air, brighter than anything else on the rock.

Hook presses forward from the right side of the composition, rapier extended level with Peter's crouched form, iron hook raised high and back — positioned for both thrust and brutal overhead strike. His great crimson coat billows behind him in a deep Hook's Crimson (#8B0000) that spreads like a wound against the Midnight Blue sky. He is tall, angular, magnificent and terrible, ringlet curls black against the moonlit horizon. His shadow stretches vast and disproportionate behind him across the wet rock and into the dark water — far larger than physics permits, the shadow of myth rather than a man.

The moon's reflection fractures in the black lagoon surrounding the rock's base — a broken silver path radiating outward in every direction. Rackham-dense crosshatching builds the shadows beneath both figures into something architectural and absolute, shadow as weight and pressure and meaning. The atmospheric foundation is Midnight Blue (#0D1B4B) throughout, with moonlight silver cutting hard across wet stone surfaces, Hook's Crimson flaring, and Peter's Viridian the only living note in the darkness.

Peter casts no shadow on the rock. This absence in this moment is its own charged statement.

CHARACTERS IN SCENE — Peter Pan: boy of about twelve, small and lithe with stored kinetic energy, beautiful slightly alien face with large luminous eyes without visible pupils, permanent tan, fierce brightening grin. Overlapping leaf-tunic in russets and bright Neverland Viridian, red-feathered cap, barefoot, small dagger extended forward. No shadow present. Captain Hook: tall, cadaverous, magnificently sinister, deep crimson velvet coat with tarnished gold brocade, miraculously white lace cravat, jet-black ringlet curls, iron hook catching moonlight, rapier extended. Disproportionately large shadow. Angular frame dominating his side of the composition.

ART STYLE: Edwardian ink-and-wash, Arthur Rackham and Edmund Dulac-inspired, organic gestural linework, Rackham crosshatching building dimensional shadow, rich ink-heavy darks, Midnight Blue and cold silver moonlight as dominant palette with Viridian and Crimson as figure anchors, organic panel borders dissolving into dark water at every edge.
