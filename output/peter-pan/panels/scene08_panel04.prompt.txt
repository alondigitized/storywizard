A whisper of Neverland Viridian in the curtain's folds.

The sky beyond is an empty flat grey-blue — no arc of light, no Tinker Bell Gold. Her expression is composed and private, eyes resting on the empty sky without urgency, the attentive-but-composed baseline of her adult face. Light in the room is flatter, more ordinary — diffuse grey daylight rather than amber candlelight. Linework visibly firmer than the left vignette, edges beginning to harden into London's architectural precision. Wendy Periwinkle (#7B9DC4) entering her palette.
