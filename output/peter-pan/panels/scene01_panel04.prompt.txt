IMAGE PROMPT:
Tall narrow vertical panel composition, viewed from inside the darkened nursery looking toward the window. Peter Pan fills the frame, crouched on the exterior window ledge, one bare hand gripping the wooden frame, body suspended between London and the room — weight coiled, ready to spring backward into the night. The camera sits at mid-room height, slightly below Peter's eye level, so he looms against the open sky. He faces inward, his gaze just having landed on the sleeping figure below frame — and that landing has changed something. His chin still tilts with instinctive cockiness, but his wide luminous eyes have narrowed by a fraction, calculating. The grin hasn't left his mouth but it has acquired an edge — the smile of a wild animal deciding whether to bolt. Every muscle in his crouched body is a coiled question.

Behind him, London breathes: dark Edwardian rooftops recede into middle distance, chimney stacks exhaling pale smoke that drifts across a slice of deep moonlit sky — Midnight Blue (#0D1B4B) and silver, cold and infinite. The city is beautiful from this impossible perch. He belongs to that sky and the night clearly knows it. The interior side of the frame pulls the Nursery Amber warmth inward — a soft amber glow from below catches the underside of his leaf-tunic, the soles of his bare feet, the scratched knuckles of his grip hand — the only warmth touching him, coming from a room he hasn't decided to enter.

His shadow is absent. Where his body should cast a shadow across the window frame and sill, there is only clean, uninterrupted painted wood. This absence is quiet and deliberate — an uncanny negative space more unsettling than any monster.

The panel border is the key transitional signal: the left and bottom edges are straight and architectural in London's precise visual grammar, but the top and right edges — the sides nearest the open sky and Peter — soften and grow slightly irregular, the linework breathing, the border beginning to behave like Neverland.

CHARACTERS IN SCENE — Peter Pan: A boy of about twelve, small and lithe, assembled from stored kinetic energy. Face beautiful and slightly alien: large luminous eyes without visible pupils, sharp chin, mouth perpetually near mischief. Permanent outdoor tan. Tunic of overlapping autumn leaves and forest materials — russets, deep greens, umber browns, spider-silk stitching — organic and irregular. Small red-feathered cap. Always barefoot. No shadow present. Small dagger tucked into leaf-tunic, barely visible. Scratched knuckles. His dominant color: Neverland Viridian (#1A7A5E) with amber undertow from the nursery below.

ART STYLE — Studio Ghibli-inspired watercolor illustration: soft luminous watercolor washes, delicate hand-drawn linework in the manner of Miyazaki and Kazuo Oga. Atmospheric backgrounds with meticulous detail. Characters with simple expressive faces and large emotive eyes. Deep indigos and silvers for night. The world feels alive, layered, handpainted — nostalgic wonder carrying the undertow of loss.
