Medium close-up at lagoon water level, night. The dark, glassy lagoon fills the lower two-thirds of the frame — deep Midnight Blue water scattered with tiny silver ripples catching moonlight in trembling crescents. The camera sits almost at the surface, horizon line low, indigo sky dissolving into distant stars above.

Peter Pan floats just beyond the shadow of a great dark rock, only his face and the tops of his shoulders breaking the surface. He is grinning — wide, reckless, incandescent — the grin of a boy who has just discovered the most magnificent thing he could possibly do at this precise moment. One finger pressed to his lips, angled toward the viewer: pure, gleeful conspiracy. His luminous eyes catch the moonlight and gleam with impish light. Water beads at his jaw; his autumn-leaf tunic darkens where it is wet. No shadow falls from him onto the water — the surface beneath him shimmers uninterrupted, as though he weighs nothing at all.

To the right, barely separated from darkness: Wendy's pale face emerges from the shadow of a clustered rock formation. Only her face and the very edge of her shoulder are visible — she holds herself perfectly still, barely breathing, large watchful eyes fixed on Peter with an expression suspended between terror, disbelief, and something that is almost, unwillingly, delight. Her nightgown's white collar catches a ghost of reflected moonlight. The rest of her is shadow.

The lagoon atmosphere is Night Neverland at its most unearthly — Midnight Blue water, Fairy Dust Silver moonlight diffused across the surface, and just below the waterline the faintest suggestion of bioluminescent coral glowing pale turquoise. Rock formations loom in silhouette, fractionally too large, the scale of childhood memory. The panel border is organic and irregular, the nearest rock interrupting the right frame edge.

CHARACTERS IN SCENE — Peter Pan: boy of about twelve, small and lithe, permanently tanned skin, luminous eyes without visible pupils in close-up, sharp chin, mouth wide in reckless grin. Tunic of overlapping autumn leaves in russets and deep greens darkened by water. Small red-feathered cap. No shadow on the water beneath him. Scratched hands just visible at the surface. Neverland Viridian as dominant color even in night palette; Fairy Dust Silver catching at his wet shoulders. Wendy Darling: girl of thirteen or fourteen, pale face emerging from rock shadow, large watchful eyes wide with held breath, white nightgown collar just visible at throat. Wendy Periwinkle palette cooled toward silver in the night scene.

Night palette of deep Midnight Blue, cool indigo, and Fairy Dust Silver. Water rendered as breathing, shimmering life. Characters with simple expressive faces and large emotive eyes.
