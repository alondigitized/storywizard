IMAGE PROMPT:
Medium close-up at exact water level, the camera almost touching the lagoon surface. The dark, glassy water of the Mermaids' Lagoon fills the lower two-thirds of the panel, its surface a near-perfect mirror broken only by tiny, concentric moonlit ripples radiating outward from Peter's stillness. The night sky and the jagged silhouettes of volcanic rock formations are faintly reflected in the water's dark surface.

Peter Pan floats at the center-left of the frame, only his face and the tops of his leaf-tunic shoulders rising above the waterline. His expression is the heart of the image: a wide, radiant, entirely reckless grin — not the grin of a boy who has been brave, but of a boy who has just thought of something magnificent and cannot contain the magnificence. His luminous eyes gleam with impish, interior light, catching the cold moonlight. His left hand — fingers scratched from old battles — rises from the water, one finger pressed to his lips in a conspiratorial gesture directed toward the viewer, drawing us into the scheme. He is half-submerged, half-illuminated, a boy who belongs equally to water and sky.

To the right, half-hidden in the deep ink-shadows of a rock cluster, Wendy's pale face is barely visible — only the whites of her wide eyes and the oval of her pale cheek catching the moonlight. She is watching, barely breathing, nightgown indistinct against shadow.

The lighting is Neverland night: Midnight Blue (#0D1B4B) dominates the sky and depths, with cold moonlight raking low and silver across the water's surface, casting Peter's face in luminous half-light. The ripples around him catch the moon in brief, scattered gleams of Fairy Dust Silver (#C8D8E8). The rock shadows are ink-heavy, built with crosshatching, dense enough to swallow Wendy entirely except for those watching eyes. There is no warm amber here — only moon, shadow, and the cool green of Neverland Viridian in the faint luminescence clinging to Peter's tunic beneath the waterline. Peter casts no shadow on the water — his reflection is subtly wrong, slightly misaligned.

The panel border is organic and irregular — the rock to the right interrupts the frame edge, and the dark water bleeds slightly beyond its boundary, Neverland refusing containment.

Mood: giddy, conspiratorial, suspended — the held breath before a magnificent, irresponsible thing is done.

Wendy: Pale-faced, large watchful eyes, white nightgown. Face barely resolved from rock shadow — wide-eyed, utterly still.

ART STYLE: Edwardian ink-and-wash with luminous watercolor washes — Rackham and Dulac linework, organic and gestural. Dark fairy tale aesthetic, rich ink shadows, crosshatched depth. Neverland night palette: Midnight Blue dominant, Silver and Viridian accents. Slightly oversaturated, cherished-memory quality. Organic panel borders.
